About

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Sheffield and a member of the Natural Language Processing Group, supervised by Prof. Nikos Aletras and Dr Zheng Yuan.

My research focuses on natural language processing, language model evaluation, and agentic AI, with a particular interest in legal applications and other high-stakes domains. I study how language models and AI agents reason, make decisions, and collaborate, and how to evaluate their capabilities and limitations. More broadly, I am interested in developing methods that improve the reliability and interpretability of these systems, and exploring how they can support human expertise and decision-making. My work aims to connect advances in AI with the practical demands of settings where accuracy, accountability, and human judgment matter.

I welcome collaborations with researchers and practitioners across disciplines on these topics and related challenges in high-stakes domains. If you see a connection with your work or have an idea to explore, please feel free to get in touch.

Previously, I completed the MPhil in Advanced Computer Science at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Prof. Simone Teufel and Dr Olesya Razuvayevskaya. I received my Master of Data Science from the University of Sydney, where I worked with Prof. Seokhee Hong.
